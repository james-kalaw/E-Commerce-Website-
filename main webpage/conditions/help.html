FAQs

What is SoftWhere?

SoftWhere is a digital marketplace offering subscriptions and access to popular tools
and platforms, including streaming services, productivity apps, creative design software, and more.



How do I purchase a product?

Browse our catalog, add your chosen product to the cart, and complete the checkout process.
Once payment is confirmed, you'll receive access details via email.



Are there refunds?

Refunds are granted on a case-by-case basis. For example, if you experience technical issues that
prevent product activation, our support team will assist you in resolving the matter or processing a refund.



Can I share my subscription?

Sharing subscription credentials is against our
Terms of Service and may lead to account suspension without a refund.



What payment methods do you accept?

We accept a variety of payment methods, including major credit cards,
PayPal, and other secure online payment gateways.



How secure is my personal information?

We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data and comply with data privacy
laws to ensure your personal information is safe.



What should I do if I encounter a problem?

Visit our Help Center to find troubleshooting guides or contact
our support team via live chat or email for assistance.
